Dr. Norman Borlaug

A Life Dedicated to Fighting Hunger

Dr. Norman Borlaug was an American agronomist and humanitarian who helped develop improved wheat varieties and agricultural techniques that increased food production in many countries.

His work contributed to the Green Revolution, which helped millions of people gain access to more reliable food supplies. In 1970, he received the Nobel Peace Prize for his contributions to increasing the world's food supply.

Important Achievements

  • Developed high-yielding, disease-resistant wheat varieties.
  • Helped improve agricultural production in developing countries.
  • Received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1970.
  • Dedicated much of his life to reducing hunger and poverty.

Norman Borlaug's legacy reminds us that science, dedication, and international cooperation can make a meaningful difference in people's lives.
