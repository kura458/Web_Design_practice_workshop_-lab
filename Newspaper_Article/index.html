Technology Is Transforming Education

Digital learning opens new opportunities for students

By Kura Lemma

Technology is changing the way students learn, communicate, and share knowledge. Digital classrooms, online resources, and interactive tools help learners explore subjects beyond traditional textbooks.

Educators are also discovering new ways to support students through technology. When digital tools are combined with effective teaching, they can improve access to information and encourage lifelong learning.
